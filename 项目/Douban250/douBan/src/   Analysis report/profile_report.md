Douban Top250 Movie Data Analysis

• Analyzed Douban Top250 movie dataset to identify rating patterns, genre differences, and temporal trends.

• Performed data cleaning and preprocessing, including handling missing values and extracting movie categories.

• Conducted statistical analysis to evaluate rating distribution, including mean, skewness, and hypothesis testing.

• Discovered that serious genres such as documentary and history tend to have higher ratings, while entertainment-oriented genres like crime and family show relatively lower ratings.

• Identified that niche genres have higher average ratings despite smaller sample sizes, while mainstream genres have more stable ratings due to larger audience bases.

• Visuali zed trends using line charts and bar charts to illustrate category distribution and rating changes over time.
