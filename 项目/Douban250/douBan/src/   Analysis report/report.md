Douban Top250 Movie Analysis

This dataset contains the top 250 movies from Douban, including ratings, categories and release years.

Serious genres such as documentary and history tend to have higher ratings, while more entertainment-oriented genres like crime and family tend to have relatively lower ratings.

Although documentary movies have a smaller number of samples, their average ratings are relatively high, while drama movies have a larger number but more stable ratings.

This may be because niche genres have a smaller audience size, while mainstream genres have a larger audience size.
